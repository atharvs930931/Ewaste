About Us

Responsible E-Waste Management for a Cleaner Future

Who We Are

The E-Waste Portal is a digital platform designed to manage electronic waste efficiently and responsibly. It connects users, recyclers, and administrators in one transparent system to ensure safe recycling and environmental protection.

Our Mission

  • Ensure proper disposal of electronic waste
  • Reduce pollution caused by e-waste
  • Connect users and recyclers digitally
  • Provide real-time tracking
  • Promote recycling awareness

Our Vision

  • Zero E-Waste Pollution
  • Sustainable solutions for smart cities
  • Paperless and transparent system
  • Long-term environmental responsibility

How the Portal Works

User registers & submits E-Waste

Recycler accepts the request

Status tracking (Pending / Accepted / Completed)

Safe & eco-friendly recycling

Why Choose Our Platform

  • Easy-to-use interface
  • Photo & location-based submission
  • Real-time status tracking
  • Secure role-based access
  • Environment-friendly solution

Impact on Environment

  • Reduction in pollution
  • Proper recycling awareness
  • Green & clean initiative
  • Responsible disposal culture
